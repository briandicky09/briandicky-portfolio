Nalar

2026

An AI-powered personalized learning companion built with Next.js, React 19, TypeScript, and Framer Motion that extracts university lecture PDFs, diagnoses conceptual gaps, and guides students like a private tutor.

Nalar is an interactive AI study workspace designed for university students navigating dense academic syllabi and lecture slide decks. Rather than giving generic summaries, Nalar focuses on deep conceptual mastery.

Students upload course PDFs into their Nalar Workspace, where the platform maps core topics, evaluates comprehension through adaptive questioning, pinpoints specific conceptual weaknesses, and delivers structured explanations tailored to the curriculum.

I crafted the frontend architecture using Next.js App Router, React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and Framer Motion, creating a distraction-free editorial study environment with fluid transitions and real-time mastery progress indicators.

Challenge

University students studying complex lecture PDFs often rely on passive reading without knowing which core concepts they misunderstand or how to bridge those knowledge gaps effectively.

Solution

I built an interactive Next.js and React 19 study platform that parses course PDFs into structured modules, actively diagnoses comprehension weaknesses, and provides guided Socratic tutoring with smooth Framer Motion choreography.
